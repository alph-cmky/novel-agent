# Style Rules

Measure prose; do not prescribe prose mechanically. Style findings are evidence, not verdicts. Semantic literary judgment belongs to `Editor`.

Distinguish narrative / dialogue / mixed paragraphs. Support common Chinese quotation marks (`“”`, `「」`, `『』`, `""`). Pure dialogue must not be treated as fragmented narration. Short sentences in action scenes are not automatically bad.

Isolated words (突然, 忽然, 下一秒, 就在这时) are not proof of AI writing. Do not reward keyword-based cliffhangers or use fixed global dialogue ratios as quality requirements.

Before adding a new style metric, show: the real failure pattern, why existing metrics miss it, expected effect, and false-positive risk. Prefer fewer meaningful metrics over many correlated scores.
